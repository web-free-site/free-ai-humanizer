How to edit AI text before you publish

A draft from a chatbot is a starting point, not a finished piece. It can sound generic, repeat itself, or invent details. Edit it yourself, and use Free AI Humanizer only as a way to vary wording in the browser — not as a guarantee that the text will pass any detector.

Steps

  1. Check every fact and name. Verify claims, dates, quotes, and product behavior against a source you trust. Remove anything you cannot stand behind.
  2. Delete filler and repeated structure. AI drafts often reopen the same point with new adjectives. Keep the sentences that add information.
  3. Put back your own examples. Add details only you know: the product, the place, the constraint. That is usually what a reader came for.
  4. Optional wording pass. If the tone still feels stiff, paste a short section into Free AI Humanizer and review the rewrite. Then edit again in your own words.
  5. Read it aloud before you publish. Awkward rhythm and leftover instructions to an AI model are easier to hear than to see.

Detectors and honesty

No in-browser rewrite can promise that a detector or a platform will treat the text as human-written. Do not use this tool to misrepresent authorship where the rules require disclosure.

Work in small chunks

Rewriting a whole article at once makes it harder to notice factual errors. Edit section by section.

Open Free AI Humanizer after you have checked the facts, not instead of editing.
