How to rewrite AI text for an email

Chatbots often produce emails that are too long, too formal, or vague about the next step. A useful rewrite is shorter and specific. You can do that by hand, and optionally run a passage through Free AI Humanizer, all in the browser.

Steps

  1. State the point in the first lines. Readers should see why you wrote and what you need. Cut the opening that only thanks the model's imaginary context.
  2. Replace generic phrases. Swap padding such as a generic well-wish for the actual subject, deadline, or question.
  3. Keep one clear request. If you need a reply, say what you are asking for and by when. Remove extra questions that can wait.
  4. Optional tone pass. Paste the body into Free AI Humanizer if you want a slightly more natural wording, then check that names, dates, and numbers are unchanged.

Do not send unchecked drafts

AI text can invent attachments, meetings, or facts. Read the final email as if you typed it from scratch.

Tone should match the relationship

A message to a close colleague can be shorter than a first message to a client. The tool does not know that context; you do.
